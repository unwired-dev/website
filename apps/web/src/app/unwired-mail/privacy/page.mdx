## Account and mailbox information

Your Unwired Product Account is separate from your email accounts. Google or Apple sign-in verifies who you are. Our backend stores the sign-in provider and its account identifier, device identifiers, platform and device authorization state to operate and secure your account. Google sign-in can provide your name, email address and profile picture to the device. We do not store your name or readable email address in the Product Account record.

You choose which mailboxes to connect and authorize each device separately. Provider credentials, including Gmail access and refresh tokens, stay in protected storage on that device. The device communicates directly with the mail provider to read, organize and send mail. Our backend temporarily verifies Google identity tokens for sign-in and notification routing; it does not retain Gmail mailbox access or refresh tokens.

Without sign-in we cannot provide account-based services. Without mailbox authorization we cannot access that mailbox. Declining an optional permission does not authorize a different use of your data.

## Google account and Gmail access

When you connect Gmail, Unwired Mail requests basic Google identity information and the `gmail.modify` permission. This lets the app read messages, attachments, labels and change history; search; organize mail, including archive, read state, labels and Trash; and compose, reply, forward and send mail. It also lets the device request Gmail change notifications. The app does not request the broader `mail.google.com` permission for permanent deletion.

We use Google data only to provide the mail and account features you choose. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Unwired Mail follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements, when using or transferring Google API data.

Google's Sign-In SDK also processes information under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Its privacy declarations include account/profile, device, usage and security-related information, including coarse location. Those declarations do not mean Unwired requests phone-number or location permission, or stores those details in a readable account profile.

You can revoke access in [Google Account third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revocation stops future authorized Gmail access but does not by itself delete your Unwired account or already synchronized encrypted data. To remove those, use the controls below.

## On-device processing and encrypted sync

Messages are cached encrypted on your device. Searching saved mail, categorization, summaries, translation and writing assistance run on the device. If you choose “Search mail servers”, the app sends your search query directly to the selected mail providers. The app does not send Gmail content to an external AI model service. Planned cloud assistance and product analytics are not enabled processing described by this notice.

Private Sync stores end-to-end encrypted mailbox descriptions, preferences, Drafts and Draft attachments with our hosting provider, Convex, so your Trusted Devices can use them. This can include addresses, recipients, subjects, text, quoted mail, files and photos. The backend does not hold the keys needed to read that content. Encryption does not make your account or operational records anonymous.

You and your devices hold the Recovery Key. Unwired cannot recover lost keys or decrypt your synchronized content for you.

Contacts permission is optional. The app reads contacts on the device to suggest recipients and does not upload your address book. A contact you choose as a recipient becomes part of the Draft and of the message you send.

## Notifications and sending

Google Cloud Pub/Sub delivers a Gmail mailbox address and change marker to our notification relay. The relay uses a keyed digest of the address to match the mailbox to your devices; it does not retain the readable address. It stores routing and ownership-verification information, including change markers. Apple Push Notification service delivers wakes using a device token and opaque route information. Message content is fetched and evaluated on your device; any notification preview is created there.

Undelivered Pub/Sub messages can remain queued for up to seven days. The production subscription does not retain acknowledged messages. These provider queues are separate from the relay's active routing records.

For sending and scheduled sending, the backend stores opaque Draft identifiers, timing and delivery-claim state to coordinate devices and avoid duplicate sends. The originating device submits the message to your mail provider. Recipients and their providers receive the mail and attachments you choose to send.

## Optional diagnostics and support

Error reporting is off by default. If you enable it, the app sends bounded diagnostic reports through Convex to PostHog's EU service. These contain an installation identifier, app/build/OS information, predefined error and operation codes, timings, status and bounded stack information. Reports exclude mail content, subjects, mailbox addresses, credentials and encryption keys. The installation identifier is separate from your Product Account but can link reports from the same installation.

You can turn reporting off and request deletion using the app's error-report controls. Unsent reports expire locally after 24 hours. PostHog's current free-plan event retention is one year; that is a retention window, not a promise that every copy is erased at that exact time.

If you contact us, we process your contact details and what you choose to send to answer the request and keep an appropriate record. Support email is handled through Google Workspace. Send only the information needed; support cannot recover your encryption keys or open encrypted synchronized mail.

## Retention, removal and deletion

Device data remains while the relevant account or mailbox is kept on that device, subject to cache limits. Signing out or removing device authorization clears the corresponding local credentials and cached content through the app's cleanup process. An offline or unreachable device cannot be remotely guaranteed to erase immediately.

Removing a Mailbox Connection everywhere removes its synchronized connection and routing information. It does not delete mail held by the provider. Deleting your Product Account removes its active account, device and encrypted-sync records through the deletion process. Provider revocation or deletion steps that fail can be retried; the minimum information needed to finish them can remain until that process completes.

Account deletion retains a minimal sign-in identity marker to prevent reuse of a deleted account; that marker currently has no automatic expiry. Operational delivery and routing records are retained for their account, device and delivery purposes and removed as those relationships and operations are retired. We keep support, security or legal records only for the applicable purpose or legal obligation. Hosting security logs and any backup copies follow the providers' retention and deletion cycles, so deletion from active storage does not promise immediate erasure from every infrastructure copy.

You can revoke Google access independently, remove a device's authorization, remove a mailbox everywhere, delete error reports, or delete your Product Account. These are different actions. Deleting an Unwired account does not delete your Google Account or the mail in your provider's mailbox.
